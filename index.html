ℹ️ How It Works

Understanding the examination system

🔹 For Students: Click "Start Examination", enter your name and class. You'll be taken to the CBT exam page where you answer multiple-choice questions. Your score is calculated automatically.

🔹 For Teachers: Click "Teacher Portal", enter your name and assigned class. You can manage questions, upload via CSV, enter scores, and generate report cards.

🔹 For Admins: Click "Admin Login" to access full control — manage classes, subjects, grading system, all questions, and all report cards.

🔹 Report Cards: Students and parents can view results by entering the student's name and class. Teachers generate the official report cards from their dashboard.

📞 Need Help? Contact the school admin office during school hours.
